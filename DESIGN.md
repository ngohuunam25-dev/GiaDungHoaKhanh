## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for direct-to-consumer domestic hardware, spotlighting culinary sanitation, food preservation, and modern household efficiency. Designed to convert high-velocity impulse mobile traffic from social discovery platforms into confident, repeat buyers, the aesthetic balances sterile domestic cleanliness with welcoming Vietnamese family warmth.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Functional Minimalism** with **Tactile Product Realism**:
- **Purity and Hygiene:** Pristine white surfaces against warm ivory backdrops evoke an immaculate, insect-free kitchen dining table.
- **Precision Engineering:** Subtle brushed metallic accents and ultra-crisp borders mimic the micro-perforated density and structural durability of premium 304 food-grade stainless steel.
- **Conversion Discipline:** High-contrast focal anchors steer the eye swiftly down the page—moving impulsively through bite-sized social proof, problem-solution demonstrations, tiered bundle selectors, and instant single-tap checkout modules.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional connection to food safety, culinary wellness, and metallic quality:

- **Primary (`#24564F` - Deep Teal):** Used strictly for high-priority conversion anchors, active state indicators, guarantee checkmarks, and checkout action triggers. Provides immediate visual weight and subconscious medical/sanitary authority.
- **Secondary (`#DDA65A` - Warm Gold):** Applied selectively for star ratings, flash-sale urgency tags, best-value bundle ribbons, and high-tier value props. Prevents the design from feeling overly clinical.
- **Tertiary (`#C6CDD0` - Stainless Silver):** Represents the tangible material. Applied to structural dividers, inactive icon strokes, card borders, and subtle metallic sheen gradients.
- **Neutral Primary (`#292D2E` - Charcoal):** High-legibility text color, chosen over pure black to preserve organic warmth across mobile LCD/OLED panels.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Page Canvas: `#F7F3EB` (Warm Ivory), creating soft visual comfort for evening mobile browsing.
  - Surface Containers & Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White), providing clear elevation and framing product imagery crisply.
  - Neutral Subdued Text: `#687174` (Medium Slate), used for secondary specs, delivery timelines, and disclaimers.

## Typography

Inter serves as the unified typographical engine. Its meticulously drawn diacritics guarantee flawless rendering for complex Vietnamese accent stacking (hỏi, ngã, nặng, sắc, huyền) without unintended baseline shifts or awkward glyph clipping.

- **Headlines:** Set with dense letter-spacing and substantial weights (`600` and `700`) to anchor scanning eyes on critical value propositions (e.g., "Inox 304 Kháng Khuẩn", "Chống Ruồi Muỗi Tuyệt Đối").
- **Body & Pricing:** Strict numerical tabular lining ensures promotional discounts, strike-through original prices, and countdown numbers stay geometrically aligned during live inventory shifts.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set with slightly expanded tracking to provide instantaneous readability on sticky mobile CTA banners, trust badges, and bundle dimension chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture prioritizes a **mobile-first, single-column conversion funnel** optimized for TikTok and social media in-app browsers, expanding to a balanced multi-column layout on desktop viewports.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Strict 4-column fluid grid. Canvas outer margin is `1rem` (16px), with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Maximizes screen real estate for direct comparison charts, review carousels, and persistent bottom checkout drawers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins. Side-by-side product feature and video breakdown.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width bound to `1120px` to maintain strict optical focus without dispersing conversion intent.
- **Rhythm Rules:** Inter-component vertical rhythm strictly sticks to 8px base increments. Form field clusters use `space-sm` (8px), card interior pads utilize `space-md` (16px), and overarching sales story blocks are segregated by `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp, structural containment that replicates sterile, pristine culinary environments:

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** The Warm Ivory (`#F7F3EB`) background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Elevation):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards resting directly on Level 0, bound by a crisp 1px perimeter border in Stainless Silver (`#C6CDD0`). Depth is communicated through contrast rather than heavy blur.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** Bundle selector cards and unselected option pills use a micro-ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(36, 86, 79, 0.05)`, tinted subtly with primary Deep Teal.
- **Level 3 (Overlay & Sticky CTAs):** Sticky mobile bottom sheets, modal reviews, and floating conversion trays utilize an extended diffused shadow: `0 -4px 20px rgba(41, 45, 46, 0.08)`, paired with a top border in `#C6CDD0` to preserve separation from flowing content.

## Shapes

The roundedness language follows Level `2` (Rounded), generating balanced corner radii that soften kitchen utility without appearing childish:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Ensures high compatibility with 1px metallic borders without pixel anti-aliasing distortion.
- **Large Panels & Modals:** `1rem` (16px) radius for top sheets and primary product gallery viewports.
- **Interactive Badges & Selection Chips:** `9999px` (Full Pill) for single-tap size selectors (e.g., "Đường kính 60cm", "Đường kính 70cm"), bundle quantity counters, and flash-sale countdown badges to maximize touch affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Instant Buy / Sticky CTA):** Deep Teal background (`#24564F`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border radius, minimum height of 52px for thumb precision. Active state applies a tactile scale compression of `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Add to Cart / View Details):** Pure white background, 1.5px border in `#24564F`, Deep Teal text.
- **Urgency Floating Bar:** Fixed mobile viewport bottom bar housing countdown timer, pricing with strikethrough, and a full-width Primary Button.

### Bundle Selector Cards
- Stacked pricing cards allowing 1-click selection of product combinations (e.g., "1 Chiếc", "Combo 2 Chiếc - Tiết kiệm 20%").
- Default State: White surface, 1px border in `#C6CDD0`.
- Selected State: White surface, 2px border in `#24564F`, accompanied by a top-right Warm Gold badge (`#DDA65A`) labeling "Bán chạy nhất" (Best Seller).

### Chips & Badges
- **Guarantee Badges:** Pill-shaped, light teal tint (`rgba(36, 86, 79, 0.08)`), Deep Teal text and verified check icon.
- **Specification Chips:** Stainless Silver outline, Charcoal text, displaying dimensions and steel grades (`Inox 304`, `Mắt lưới 0.8mm`).

### Form Fields (Quick Checkout)
- Input containers feature a 50px tap target, white fill, 1px border `#C6CDD0`, and Charcoal text. Focus state transitions border to Deep Teal `#24564F` with a soft 3px primary glow ring (`rgba(36, 86, 79, 0.15)`). Optimized for Vietnamese auto-fill (Họ tên, Số điện thoại, Địa chỉ nhận hàng).

### Social Proof & Review Cards
- Card surfaces feature 5-star aggregate indicators in Warm Gold (`#DDA65A`), customer verified purchase tags ("Đã mua hàng"), and integrated video/image thumbnails showing the cover deployed over real Vietnamese family meal spreads.
